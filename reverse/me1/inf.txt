                          CONCLUSION
==

Intel ME v1.x on ICH9M chipset implements NO meaningful firmware signature
verification. The combination of:
  - Empty key storage (FOVD)
  - 64-bit weak hash instead of RSA/ECDSA
  - No public keys anywhere in firmware
  - Uncompressed early boot modules

...makes firmware tampering trivially achievable without hardware programmer.

This is likely due to ME v1.x being an early implementation before Intel
implemented proper Secure Boot / Boot Guard mechanisms in later versions, or intel is goooners.
